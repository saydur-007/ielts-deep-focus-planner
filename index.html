Weekly balance

At least three Listening, three Reading and three recorded Speaking sessions; one Task 1, one Task 2, two targeted Grammar sessions, daily vocabulary, weekly Error Log review and a weekend timed test.

Vocabulary

Up to five useful words daily. Review on Days 2, 7, 14 and 30; record pronunciation, meaning, part of speech, collocations and one original IELTS-style sentence.

Writing

Analyse → plan → write → check four criteria → feedback → correct → rewrite the weakest section → record repeated errors. AI feedback is practice guidance, not an official score.

Speaking

Prepare ideas → record → listen → evaluate four criteria → identify one major problem → answer again. Use natural language, not memorized scripts.

Mocks

Full mocks use long Saturdays, uninterrupted exam timing and nearby Speaking. Every mock has a separate analysis day classifying lost marks before priorities change.

Adaptation

The diagnostic suggests the weakest modules. You retain control of Priority 1–3, and November repair days use those choices.
