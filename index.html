How this plan works

Daily rhythm

6:00–7:30 AM: 50 words, 15-min video recording, 30-min book.
On the go: 20-min listening on the office journey (any time on Fri/Sat holidays).
6:00–10:00 PM: one 3-hour deep block on a single area, then 1 page free-hand writing and a note recap.

One area at a time

Until 13 Nov you finish one course area fully before starting the next. Hand-write notes for every video — those notes become your revision book.

From 14 Nov: test + revise

Every day: one timed module test, a deep mistake analysis, then revision of two other modules from your hand notes. Listening gets extra test days because it is currently your lowest band.

Mocks

Do each mock in exam order without pausing. The next day is a full analysis day: classify every lost mark and add it to the Error Log.
