Resource order

Use Munzereen Shahid's IELTS course first, your online mock platform second, and Cambridge/official sources only where they add value.

Weekly balance

At least three Listening, three Reading and three recorded Speaking sessions; one Task 1, one Task 2, two targeted Grammar sessions, daily vocabulary, weekly Error Log review and a weekend timed test.

Vocabulary

Up to five useful words daily. Review on Days 2, 7, 14 and 30; record pronunciation, meaning, part of speech, collocations and one original IELTS-style sentence.

Mocks

Saturday is the main testing day. Sunday is analysis day. Do not simply collect scores—classify lost marks and decide what to repair.

Writing

Analyse → plan → write → check four criteria → feedback → correct → rewrite the weakest section → record repeated errors.

Speaking

Prepare ideas → record → listen → evaluate four criteria → identify one major problem → answer again. Use natural language, not memorized scripts.
